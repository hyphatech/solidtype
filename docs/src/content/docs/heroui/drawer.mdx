`Solidtype_heroui.Drawer` is Kobalte's `Dialog` sliding in from an edge of
the page, with the classes HeroUI's own `drawerVariants` gives. It is a
[Modal](/solidtype/heroui/modal/) in all but these: its content slides in
from the edge its `placement` names; its trigger is a button; and while it
may be dismissed its dialog is dragged toward its edge to close, as HeroUI's
is.

## Anatomy

```ocaml
<Drawer>
  <Button>"Open Drawer"</Button>
  <Drawer.Backdrop>
    <Drawer.Content placement=`bottom>
      <Drawer.Dialog>
        <Drawer.Handle />
        <Drawer.Close_trigger />
        <Drawer.Header>
          <Drawer.Heading>...</Drawer.Heading>
        </Drawer.Header>
        <Drawer.Body>...</Drawer.Body>
        <Drawer.Footer>...</Drawer.Footer>
      </Drawer.Dialog>
    </Drawer.Content>
  </Drawer.Backdrop>
</Drawer>
```

### Global CSS

HeroUI's classes are global, so a stylesheet restyles every one, as with
HeroUI in React:

```css
@layer components {
  .drawer__dialog {
    @apply rounded-t-3xl;
  }

  .drawer__heading {
    @apply text-lg font-semibold;
  }
}
```

- **Entering**: `[data-entering="true"]` on the backdrop and the content
- **Exiting**: `[data-exiting="true"]` on the backdrop and the content
- **Placement**: `[data-placement="bottom"]`, and the other edges, on the
  content and the dialog

## How it is made

It shares Modal's making, on Kobalte's dialog: see
[Modal](/solidtype/heroui/modal/#how-it-is-made). Its drag is HeroUI's: from
anywhere on its dialog but a button, a field, a link or its body, it starts
past 8 pixels, follows the pointer toward the drawer's edge alone, and on
release closes the drawer past 30% of its size or at a flick faster than half
a pixel a millisecond, leaving the drawer where it was let go to slide on out,
else springs back. The dialog's own slide in and out is waited for, as its
backdrop's and content's are.

`Solidtype_heroui.Dropdown` is Kobalte's `DropdownMenu`, with the classes
HeroUI's own `dropdownVariants`, `menuItemVariants` and `menuSectionVariants`
give. A `Button` inside it is its trigger, as in HeroUI; anything else is
wrapped in `Dropdown.Trigger`. HeroUI's `Label`, `Description`, `Header`,
`Kbd` and `Separator` go inside its items and sections, as in React.

```ocaml

  <Dropdown.Trigger>...</Dropdown.Trigger>

      </Dropdown.Item>
      <Separator />
      <Dropdown.Section>
        <Header>...</Header>
        <Dropdown.Item id="..." />
      </Dropdown.Section>
      <Dropdown.Submenu_trigger>
        <Dropdown.Item id="...">
          <Label>...</Label>
          <Dropdown.Submenu_indicator />
        </Dropdown.Item>
        <Dropdown.Popover>
          <Dropdown.Menu>
            <Dropdown.Item id="..." />
          </Dropdown.Menu>
        </Dropdown.Popover>
      </Dropdown.Submenu_trigger>
    </Dropdown.Menu>
  </Dropdown.Popover>
</Dropdown>
```

### Global CSS

HeroUI's classes are global, so a stylesheet restyles every one, as with
HeroUI in React:

```css
@layer components {
  .dropdown__trigger {
    @apply outline-none;
  }

  .dropdown__popover {
    @apply rounded-lg border border-border bg-overlay p-2;
  }

  .dropdown__menu {
    @apply flex flex-col gap-1;
  }
}
```

## Styling Reference

### CSS Classes

- `.dropdown__trigger`, `.dropdown__popover`, `.dropdown__menu`
- `.menu-item`, `.menu-item--default`, `.menu-item--danger`,
  `.menu-item__indicator`, `.menu-item__indicator--submenu`, and the
  indicator's `[data-slot="menu-item-indicator--checkmark"]` and
  `[data-slot="menu-item-indicator--dot"]`
- `.menu-section`, and `.header` for its title
- `[data-slot="separator"]` within the menu

### Interactive States

- **Hover**: `[data-hovered="true"]` on the trigger and the items
- **Focus**: `:focus-visible` or `[data-focus-visible="true"]` on the trigger
  and the items, and `[data-focused="true"]` on the item with the focus
- **Pressed**: `[data-pressed="true"]` on the trigger, also while the menu is
  open, and on the items
- **Disabled**: `[data-disabled="true"]` on the items
- **Selected**: `[data-selected="true"]` and `[aria-checked="true"]` on the
  items
- **Selection**: `[data-selection-mode="single"]` or `"multiple"` on the menu
  and its items
- **Submenu**: `[data-has-submenu="true"]` on an item that opens one, and
  `[data-open="true"]` while it is open
- **Entering** and **Exiting**: `[data-entering="true"]` and
  `[data-exiting="true"]` on the popover, while it appears and disappears
- **Placement**: `[data-placement="bottom"]`, and the other sides, on the
  popover

## How it is made

Kobalte's menu opens from its trigger, moves the focus between its items by
the arrows and by what is typed, opens a submenu by a hover or the arrow
toward it, closes on Escape or a press outside and gives the focus back to
its trigger, as React Aria's does. Its content is the menu itself, where
React Aria's popover is a dialog around its menu; a reader is told the same
menu and items, and HeroUI's styles read the same classes and attributes.

HeroUI selects in the menu or a section, by the items' ids, where Kobalte
has an item of each kind: an item of a selection of one is Kobalte's
checkbox item with the role of a radio, of many a checkbox item, each
checked while its id is selected. The menu gives the focus to its selected
item as it opens, as React Aria's does, where Kobalte's gives it to the
menu. Enter on an item of a selection of many closes the menu, and a press
or Space keeps it open, as React Aria decides.

A long press is React Aria's: the menu opens on a press held half a second,
or on Alt and an arrow, and its trigger is described so to a reader. Kobalte
opens it on any press, so the dropdown keeps its open state and refuses the
others.

`Solidtype_heroui.Time_field` is Kobalte's `TimeField.Root`, with the classes
HeroUI's own `timeFieldVariants` gives, and its group, input and segments
HeroUI's `dateInputGroupVariants`. Each part of the time -- the hour, the
minute, the second and AM or PM -- is a segment, typed into or stepped by the
arrow keys. Its label names it, its description describes it, its error shows
while it is invalid, and it submits its value with a form, by Kobalte's hidden
input.

Its value is Kobalte's `Types.Time`, an hour, a minute and a second, made by
`Solidtype_kobalte.Types.Time.make ~hour:14. ~minute:30. ()`.

### Keyboard

A segment is typed into, or stepped by the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down,
Home and End, as Kobalte's are; an arrow key on an empty segment steps it from
zero. Left and Right move between the segments, which stand in Kobalte's
order, hour, minute, second, then AM or PM, with the reader's locale's
literals between them.

### Controlled

The field's value changes, and its `on_change` is told, once every segment has
its part, as React's does; a time partly typed is the field's alone.

<Example code={ControlledCode} title="time_field_controlled.mlx"><Controlled.make client:visible /></Example>

### Granularity and Hour Cycle

`granularity` is the smallest part it shows, and `hour_cycle` its clock, else
the reader's locale's.

<Example code={GranularityCode} title="time_field_granularity.mlx"><Granularity.make client:visible /></Example>

### TimeField.Input, TimeField.Segment, TimeField.Prefix, TimeField.Suffix

their `children`.

`Solidtype_heroui.Color_swatch_picker` is a list of colour swatches one is
chosen from, with the classes HeroUI's own `colorSwatchPickerVariants` gives.
Kobalte has no such component, so it is made headless first,
[`Solidtype_headless.Color_swatch_picker`](/solidtype/kobalte/headless/), of
Kobalte's list hooks as Kobalte makes its own lists: a `listbox` of
`option`s, each named by the swatch inside it.

Tab reaches the chosen swatch, or the first. In the grid, the default, Left
and Right move to the swatch before and after, as the page's direction reads
them, and Up and Down to the one above and below in the same column; in a
stack, Up and Down move to the one before and after. Home and End reach the
ends, Page Up and Page Down too unless the list scrolls, and typing the
start of a colour's name reaches the swatch of that name. A disabled swatch
is passed over. A press, Space or Enter chooses a swatch.

Its colours are Kobalte's `Types.Color`, read from any CSS colour by
`Solidtype_kobalte.Colors.parse_color "#F43F5E"`, as HeroUI's are read by
`parseColor`. A value is its swatch's when the two are one colour, however
each is written: `rgb(139, 92, 246)` chooses `#8B5CF6`.

Original HeroUI documentation: [ColorSwatchPicker](https://heroui.com/en/docs/react/components/color-swatch-picker).

## Styling Reference

### CSS Classes

- `.color-swatch-picker`, and its parts': `.color-swatch-picker__item`,
  `.color-swatch-picker__swatch`, `.color-swatch-picker__indicator`
- its sizes, `.color-swatch-picker--xs`, `--sm`, `--md`, `--lg` and `--xl`
- its variants, `.color-swatch-picker--circle` and `--square`
- its layouts, `.color-swatch-picker--grid` and `--stack`

### Interactive States

- the list: `[data-layout]`, `grid` or `stack`
- an item: `[data-hovered="true"]`, `[data-pressed="true"]`,
  `[data-focused="true"]`, `[data-focus-visible="true"]`,
  `[data-selected="true"]`, `[data-disabled="true"]`, and its colour,
  `--color-swatch-current`
- the indicator: `[data-light-color="true"]` over a light colour, which
  draws its checkmark black

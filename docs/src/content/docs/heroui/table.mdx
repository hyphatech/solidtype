`Solidtype_heroui.Table` is HeroUI's Table, its classes HeroUI's own
`tableVariants`, around a `<table role="grid">` moved through by the keys as
React Aria's grid is. Kobalte has no table: its rows are a list of Kobalte's,
made of the hooks Kobalte's own lists are, `Solidtype_kobalte.Selection`,
which focus a row and move between rows by the arrow keys, Home and End, Page
Up and Page Down and typing; the port moves along a row's cells and between a
cell and the cell above or below it.

Rows given by data are Solid's `For` inside `Table.Body`, as HeroUI's are its
body's `items` and a render function; columns and cells given by data are a
`For` too, as HeroUI's are its header's `columns` and `Table.Collection`.

Its rows are selected by Kobalte's list, one or many, by a press, Space or
Enter, or a `Checkbox` of `slot=`selection` in a row, and all of them by one
in the header. Sorting, loading more as the table scrolls, resizing columns,
expandable rows and virtualization are being ported, each in a stage of its
own; their examples are added with them.

## Anatomy

```ocaml
<Table>
  <Table.Scroll_container>
    <Table.Content aria_label="Example table">
      <Table.Header>
        <Table.Column>"Name"</Table.Column>
        <Table.Column>"Role"</Table.Column>
      </Table.Header>
      <Table.Body>
        <Table.Row>
          <Table.Cell>"Kate Moore"</Table.Cell>
          <Table.Cell>"CEO"</Table.Cell>
        </Table.Row>
      </Table.Body>
    </Table.Content>
  </Table.Scroll_container>
  <Table.Footer />
</Table>
```

### Selection

`selection_mode` on `Table.Content` selects its rows. A `Checkbox` of
`slot=`selection` stands for its row, and in the header for every row.

### Empty State

`render_empty_state` on `Table.Body` is drawn while it has no row, across
every column.

<Example code={ExEmptyStateCode} title="table_empty_state.mlx"><ExEmptyState.make client:visible /></Example>

### TanStack Table

HeroUI's TanStack Table example drives the table with `@tanstack/react-table`,
React's; a Solid application has `@tanstack/solid-table`, which a table of
`For`s takes as it is.
